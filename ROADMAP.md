## Quality bar

A section is useful only if a beginner can learn from it without already knowing the topic. Each section should include:
1. The essential concept in plain language.
2. Rules or notation needed to apply it.
3. At least one worked example.
4. Practice questions that test understanding.
5. Links to relevant assignments or the next topic.

Do not add unrelated technologies, duplicate notes, or empty placeholder folders. Keep explanations tied to the handout and label assumptions or extra examples clearly.
